Send anonymous reports? No photos, nothing personal.

What's sent?
  • Crash reports: the error, and for a failed scan the scanner's last log lines.
  • Usage: pages opened, how long scans, processing and exports take, film type, scan resolution, frame count, whether dust removal was on.
  • Never: photos, file or folder names, your scanner's serial number. Reports carry a random ID.
