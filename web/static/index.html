Process

Turn a scanned roll into finished frames. A scan you just ran opens here by itself; to work on an older roll, open its .raw from the work folder.

  1. Open a rawScan a roll, or Open… any .raw on this computer. It is read in place, never copied, and processed right away: a preview of the whole strip, in a few seconds to a minute.
  2. Place and exportThe roll opens in Frames. Pick the Format, then press Auto-frame to put a box on every frame, or place them by hand: click the strip, Enter adds one, Delete removes one. Export saves the box positions beside the raw, so opening it again brings them back.

Privacy

Anonymous reports to the developer show what breaks on scanners other than the one Pakon was built on, and which parts of the app people use.

Never sent: your photos, file or folder names, or your scanner's serial number. Reports carry a random ID, which turning both off forgets.

Reports go to a self-hosted Rybbit (open-source analytics) run by Pakon's developer, not to a third party.

This build of Pakon doesn't send reports.

About

Help improve Pakon

Send anonymous reports so problems on other scanners get fixed. No photos, nothing personal. You can change this in Settings.

What's sent?
  • Crash reports: the error, and for a failed scan the scanner's last log lines.
  • Usage: pages opened, how long scans, processing and exports take, film type, scan resolution, frame count, whether dust removal was on.
  • Never: photos, file or folder names, your scanner's serial number. Reports carry a random ID.
