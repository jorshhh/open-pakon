What it does

The Pakon F-135 was built to scan whole rolls of 35 mm film fast, but its software only runs on Windows XP. This free app takes its place.

Works on today's computers

Mac, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux. No old PC, no virtual machine, nothing extra to set up.

Kodak's colours

The same colour processing as the original software, so your scans look the way a Pakon scan should.

Colour, black & white and slides

Colour negative, black & white (including XP2 and BW400CN) and slide film in uncut strips.

Check every frame

OpenPakon finds the pictures on the strip. Move, add or remove the boxes, including half-frame and panoramic shots.

Saves like a lab

Each roll gets its own folder with JPEGs, TIFFs, raw negatives and a contact sheet.

Knows your film

OpenPakon reads the code on the film's edge and adds the film name and ISO to every picture, like "Kodak Gold 400".

Removes dust

Optional dust and scratch removal for colour negative film (and XP2 or BW400CN), using the scanner's infrared light.

Free forever

Not a trial, not a free tier. Every feature, every roll, no catch. The code is open source, so it stays free.

From strip to finished pictures

  1. Feed the film. Pick the film type, press Scan, and feed the strip when OpenPakon says so.
  2. Check the frames. OpenPakon puts a box on every picture. Nudge any that are off.
  3. Save. Choose JPEGs, TIFFs, raw negatives or a contact sheet. Done.
