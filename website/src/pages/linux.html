Pakon scanner software for Linux

Scan with your Kodak Pakon F-135 or F-135+ on a Linux PC. Free, with nothing else to set up.

{{download:linux}}

Install

  1. Download pakon_amd64.deb above.
  2. Open it with your software installer, or in a terminal in your Downloads folder run sudo apt install ./pakon_amd64.deb
  3. Plug in the scanner with its USB cable, switch it on and open the app from your apps menu.

Then follow the guide to your first scan.

Updating

OpenPakon tells you when there's a new version. Quit OpenPakon and install the new download the same way. Your scans and settings stay where they are.
