Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon.

No more TLX in a Windows XP virtual machine

Until now, scanning with a Pakon on a Mac meant running TLXClientDemo inside a Windows XP virtual machine: buying or finding XP, setting up the virtual machine, getting the USB connection to pass through, and hoping it held for a whole roll. On Apple silicon Macs it barely works at all.

OpenPakon is a normal Mac app. Plug in the scanner, open the app and scan.

Install

  1. Download the right version for your Mac, above.
  2. Open the download and drag the app (it's called Pakon) into your Applications folder.
  3. Plug in the scanner with its USB cable and switch it on. Open the app.

Then follow the guide to your first scan.

Updating

OpenPakon tells you when there's a new version. Download it, quit OpenPakon, and drag the new one into Applications. Your scans and settings stay where they are.
