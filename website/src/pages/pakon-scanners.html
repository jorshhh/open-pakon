Kodak Pakon scanners

Which Pakon scanners work?

OpenPakon works with the F-135 and the F-135+ (Plus). Same app, same way of scanning, on Mac, Windows and Linux.

ScannerWorks with OpenPakon?
Kodak Pakon F-135Yes
Kodak Pakon F-135+ (Plus)Yes
Kodak Pakon F-235, F-335No, they're different machines inside

What you need

  • A Pakon F-135 or F-135+ and its USB cable
  • A Mac (macOS 13 or later), a Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Linux PC (Ubuntu 24.04, Linux Mint 22)
  • The free OpenPakon app. That's all: no Kodak software, no Windows XP.

Then follow the guide to your first scan.
