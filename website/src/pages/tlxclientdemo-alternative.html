Moving from TLX to OpenPakon

Here's where the things you did in TLX are in OpenPakon.

Removed dustDust removal, for colour negative and B&W C41
Scanned at Base 4 or Base 8 for speedScan page: choose the Resolution

Why switch?

  • No old computer. TLX needs Windows XP. OpenPakon runs on what you have.
  • Every frame checked before saving. See the whole strip and fix boxes in one place.
  • Rolls stay organised. Each roll gets its own folder, with the film name and ISO in every picture.
  • Free and open. No licence, and the code is public.
