Still stuck?

Ask in the Pakon F-135 group on Facebook, where most Pakon owners talk. Someone has probably seen your problem before.

If it looks like a problem with the app itself, tell us what happened and attach OpenPakon's log file. It's a plain text file that records what the scanner and the app were doing. You'll find it here:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Logs/Pakon/pakon.log
  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pakon\Logs\pakon.log
  • Linux: ~/.local/state/pakon/pakon.log
